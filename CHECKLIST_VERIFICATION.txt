======================================================================================================================
  WHAT THIS RECORD DOES NOT ESTABLISH
======================================================================================================================

  * Generalisation. Every twin and constructed attack is one we designed. A detector evaluated on its authors' attacks
  is evidence that it does what it was built to do, not that it withstands an adversary who has read the design.

  * A real plant. The twin is a simulation; its physics constants are derived from SWaT's measured operating point,
  but no result here was obtained on physical equipment.

  * The cyber branch on real data. SWaT carries no network fields, so no cyber detector can be trained or evaluated on
  it. The SWaT-trained cyber ML candidate was weak and was NOT promoted; the cyber branch is deterministic rules by
  design, and its evidence is conformance to a specification.

  * Fairness in the social sense. There are no people or protected attributes in this data. The fairness measurements
  are equal treatment across source addresses, registers and plant stages, which is the meaningful reading for a
  plant.

  * Unattended operation. Alarm counts rise over a long healthy twin run and stage coverage is uneven at small
  perturbations; both bound what the advisory layer can be trusted to do without a human.
